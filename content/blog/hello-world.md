**TL;DR:** I'm launching a place to share what I build and learn — engineering notes, practical guides, and honest write-ups of side projects. The site itself is a fully static Next.js build, costs $0 to run, and is optimized for both Google and AI search engines.

Hi, I'm Oliver. This is my corner of the internet.

For a while my notes lived in scattered places: half-finished drafts in one app, code snippets in another, and anything worth sharing stuffed into 280 characters. This blog is an attempt to fix that — to write things down properly, in public, where they might actually be useful to someone else.

## What to expect

I'm a software engineer who spends most days in TypeScript and the browser, so that's mostly what I'll write about:

- **Build logs** — how side projects actually got made, including the boring parts and the mistakes.
- **Practical guides** — things like shipping a static site that ranks well, or wiring up structured data without losing your mind.
- **Opinions, softly held** — on developer tooling, web performance, and why so many blogs look the same.

## Why this site is the way it is

A few principles shaped the build:

1. **No server, no bill.** Every page is pre-rendered HTML on a free CDN. It loads fast everywhere, and it costs nothing to run.
2. **Findable by humans and machines.** Clean URLs, RSS, sitemap, structured data, and a plain-markdown `llms.txt` so AI assistants can read the site too.
3. **Writing in files, not forms.** Posts are Markdown files in a git repo. That means version history, offline writing, and no lock-in.

If you're curious how the pieces fit together, I wrote about [the full stack behind this site](/blog/how-this-site-is-built) in a separate post.
